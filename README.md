# Thirst Trail: The Last Well 💧

A 2D adventure game inspired by **charity: water**. Explore the valley, solve challenges, collect supplies, and help bring safe water back to a village.

## How to play

- Open `index.html` in your browser, or play through GitHub Pages once published.
- Use **WASD** or **arrow keys** to move, **E** or **Space** to interact, **Shift** to sprint, and **P** to pause. On mobile, use the on-screen controls.
- Talk to the Village Elder, find repair supplies, and fix the water pump. Explore the river and spring for extra challenges.
- Earn Impact points and manage your energy to complete the mission.

**Built with:** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

*This is an independent game inspired by charity: water, not an official charity: water product. Water-safety scenarios are simplified for gameplay.*
